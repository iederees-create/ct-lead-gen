# Listing Disclosures — Precision Laser Website Template

These disclosures must appear in the listing (already folded into
`final-description.txt`, repeated here as a standalone compliance checklist).

- **Digital download / no physical item.** This listing is for digital files
  only. No hardware, materials, or physical goods ship.
- **AI disclosure.** The template's code and copy were developed with the
  assistance of AI coding tools. Buyers receive a standalone
  `05-AI-DISCLOSURE-AND-NOTICES.txt` file with full detail.
- **Refund wording, subject to applicable law.** Because this is an
  immediately-downloadable digital product, refund eligibility follows
  Etsy's digital-goods policy and applicable consumer-protection law — not
  an unconditional guarantee.
- **Quote Planner disclaimer (non-binding estimate).** "The planner provides
  preliminary guidance only. Final pricing depends on file quality, machine
  setup, material availability, job complexity, finishing, quantity,
  labour, delivery and final review." This exact wording appears in the
  template's `disclaimer.html` page and must not be diluted in listing copy.
- **Fictional demo content.** The demo business name, contact details, and
  workshop-safety statement are fictional and clearly labelled as sample
  content requiring replacement before real use.
- **No unsupported claims.** No customer ratings, review counts, or
  completed-project counts appear anywhere in the product or listing
  copy. No safety/regulatory certification (ISO, CE, SABS, etc.) is
  claimed. No "same-day," "guaranteed," "bestseller," or "#1" language is
  used.
- **No medical/cosmetic laser confusion.** The listing explicitly states
  this template is for laser cutting, engraving, signage, and fabrication
  businesses, and explicitly states it is not for medical, cosmetic, or
  treatment laser businesses.
- **Licence boundaries stated.** One end-business per licence; no resale of
  the template itself as a competing product.
