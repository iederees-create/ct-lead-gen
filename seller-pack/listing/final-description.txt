HOW THE LASER CUT & ENGRAVING QUOTE PLANNER WORKS
1. Visitor picks a service type and material.
2. Visitor enters thickness, size (in their preferred unit), and quantity.
3. Visitor answers cut complexity, file readiness, and finish.
4. The planner shows the approximate material area, a service checklist, a file-preparation checklist, any missing information, a complexity indicator, and — if enabled — a preliminary budget range.
5. The visitor copies, prints, or downloads the summary, or sends it via WhatsApp/email after reviewing the exact message in an on-screen confirmation step.
