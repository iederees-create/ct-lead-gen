# FAQ — Precision Laser Website Template

1. **Is this a physical product?** No. Digital files only, delivered instantly after purchase.
2. **Is hosting included?** No. You'll need your own static hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, or traditional hosting all work).
3. **Is a domain included?** No.
4. **Is this a WordPress theme?** No — it's a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site with no build step required for ordinary use.
5. **Can I change the company name and contact details?** Yes, entirely through the central `site-config.js` file — no HTML editing needed.
6. **Can I change colours/themes?** Yes. Three built-in themes are included: Graphite Laser, Cyan Blueprint, and Amber Workshop.
7. **Can I change the materials and rates?** Yes. Every material, rate, and pricing multiplier used by the Quote Planner is editable in `site-config.js`.
8. **Does the planner give an exact final price?** No. It provides preliminary quote guidance only. Final pricing depends on file quality, machine setup, material availability, job complexity, finishing, quantity, labour, delivery and final review.
9. **Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically to customers?** No. It opens a WhatsApp deep link with a pre-filled, structured message for the visitor to review and send themselves, after an on-screen confirmation step. It never sends automatically.
10. **Does it store customer data on a server?** No. The site is entirely client-side with no backend or database. The only thing saved in the visitor's browser is their chosen colour theme.
11. **Can I use this for a client's business?** Yes, for one end-business per licence. See the included licence file for full terms.
12. **Can I resell the template itself?** No — the licence covers building one end-business website, not reselling the template as a product.
13. **Can I hide the price estimate and only collect quote briefs?** Yes. Set `planner.budgetEnabled` to `false` in `site-config.js` and the numeric budget range is hidden while the rest of the planner (checklists, summary, next steps) keeps working.
14. **Will this work for a medical or cosmetic laser business?** No. This template is built for laser cutting, engraving, signage, and fabrication businesses — not medical, cosmetic, or treatment lasers.
15. **What if I need a refund?** Contact us via Etsy messages with your order details. Because this is a digital download, refund eligibility follows Etsy's digital-goods policy and applicable consumer law.
