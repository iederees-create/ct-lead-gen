PRIVACY POSTURE

This template is entirely client-side. It does not include a backend
server or database. The Quote Planner and quote form process all input
locally in the visitor's browser; the only data persisted anywhere is
the visitor's selected colour theme, stored in their own browser's
local storage. No customer data is transmitted to or stored by the
template itself — see privacy.html in the template for the full text
you should review and adapt for your business's actual data practices.

QUOTE PLANNER ACCURACY NOTICE

The Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner performs area, complexity, and
budget calculations based entirely on the rates and multipliers you
configure in site-config.js. It has been tested (38 automated unit
tests) to never produce NaN, Infinity, or negative figures, but it
cannot verify that your configured rates are commercially accurate —
that responsibility remains yours.
