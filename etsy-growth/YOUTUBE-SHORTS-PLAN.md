# YouTube Shorts Plan — NextGenWebs

**Goal:** Week 3 external traffic + evergreen search on YouTube.  
**Length:** 12–30 seconds preferred for Shorts.  
**Links:** Exact Etsy product URL in description (not only shop home).  
**Baseline:** Social media visits = 0 — first Shorts are about **starting the graph**, not virality guarantees.

---

## Channel positioning

NextGenWebs: digital downloads for local business websites, quote planners, and practical data tools.  
No medical treatment content. No “get rich with Etsy” claims.
