# Dubbing Plan — Future Locales (Defer Most Spend)

**Principle:** Prove English winners first. Dub only high-performing scripts.  
**Do not** mass-dub all queue rows on day one.

---

## Priority languages (suggested order)

| Order | Language | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | English (primary) | Current market for Etsy + portfolio |
| 2 | Afrikaans | SA-friendly adjacency for local service templates |
| 3 | Simplified Chinese | Optional later for digital product reach |
| 4 | Spanish | Optional later for broader Etsy audience |

Exact market choice is a human business decision—not a credit burn plan.

---

## What to dub later (only after English metrics)

| Asset class | Dub? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Winning Etsy 15s VO (top 3 SKUs) | Yes | High reuse |
| Winning Shorts hooks | Yes | Short, cheap relative value |
| Portfolio 45s (top 2) | Maybe | Longer = more credit |
| Brand homepage | Yes once | One brand film |
| Cold outreach templates | Careful | Must sound native; prefer local speaker |
| Blog summaries | Low priority | Long; SEO still English-first |
| Full script catalogue | **No** | Waste |

---

## Dubbing process

1. Lock English approved take (`-v1` or later approved).  
2. Translate with human review (not raw MT alone for commercial claims).  
3. Keep disclaimers accurate in target language.  
4. Filename: `{slug}-{use-case}-{variant}-{lang}-v1.mp3` e.g. `tiling-etsy-video-voiceover-af-v1.mp3`  
5. Do not clone a specific person’s voice for “localisation.”  

---

## Credit caution

Long portfolio + blog dublines multiply cost quickly. Cap experimental dubbing to **5 files** until one non-English channel shows measured traffic.
