## Summary 1 — Laser cutting website quote planner

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Script title** | Blog audio — laser quote requests |
| **Related article** | How to Build a Laser Cutting Website That Gets Better Quote Requests |
| **Duration** | ~120s |
| **Word count** | ~280 |
| **Filename** | `blog-summary-laser-quote-planner-v1.mp3` |

**Script:**

Laser cutting and engraving shops get the same message every week: how much to laser this—with a blurry photo and almost no detail. That is not a quote request. It’s missing material, thickness, size, quantity, process type, and file readiness.

A strong laser business website does more than show a gallery. It teaches visitors what a usable brief looks like. Vector files matter because cut paths need geometry, not screenshots. Cutting, engraving, marking, and multi-step fabrication are different jobs—your site should help people self-select.

A structured quote planner improves lead quality by asking production questions before chat. WhatsApp can still be the channel—as long as the message arrives prefilled with structure. Mobile layout matters because shop owners and customers both live on phones.

Preliminary estimates are not final prices. Artwork quality, nesting, finishing, and capacity change the number. Local SEO basics still help: consistent business info, honest photos, clear service language—without fake ratings.

If you want to see this approach packaged as a template, explore the Precision Laser project demo when the link is verified. Any purchase link should be the exact product URL when it exists—not a generic shop homepage. This summary is educational; it does not guarantee traffic or sales.
