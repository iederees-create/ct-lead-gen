## Core script (≈65s)

NextGenWebs builds practical digital products for real operators—local service businesses, makers, and people who ship useful software.

We design website templates that do more than look pretty. Construction and tiling sites that structure quote requests. Pest control and solar sites that make services obvious on a phone. Wellness and skincare templates that market calmly—without medical theatre. Fabrication tools like a laser quote planner that ask for material and files before the price chat.

We also ship serious project work: analytics dashboards for CSV-driven teams, and educational data labs you can actually explain.

What we don’t do: guarantee rankings, invent reviews, or promise overnight income. Demo brands are fictional samples. Planners and calculators are preliminary guidance—not binding quotes.

If you need a template you can rebrand, a demo you can trust, or a custom build with clear scope—start with the work on this site. Explore the projects. Test a live demo. When a product is listed, use the exact product link—not a generic shop page.

NextGenWebs. Useful by design.
