# Cold Outreach Voice Notes — Permissioned, Human, Short

**Purpose:** Optional personalised voice notes for warm or carefully targeted outreach to business owners and designers.  
**Not for:** Robocalls, SMS blasts, scraped lead spam, auto-dialers.  
**Voice:** nextgenwebs-house-male-v1 — slower, personal, lower energy than ads  
**Duration:** 20–35 seconds  

Always send only where the recipient expects or permits contact. Prefer existing relationships, inbound leads, or communities that allow promotion.

---

## Rules

1. Name the problem, not the hype.  
2. Offer a demo or portfolio link—not a hard close.  
3. No guaranteed leads/income.  
4. No fake “quick question” bait if you’re pitching.  
5. Leave a clear opt-out: “Happy to leave it if it’s not relevant.”  
6. Use placeholders for URLs until known.

## Personalisation tips (do not generate as bulk spam)

- Mention a real public detail about their business in text; keep VO mostly evergreen.  
- Prefer text-first + optional VO attachment.  
- Cap daily sends manually.
