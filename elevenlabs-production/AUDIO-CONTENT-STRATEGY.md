# Audio Content Strategy — NextGenWebs (ElevenLabs)

**Owner:** NextGenWebs  
**Channel:** ElevenLabs credit balance → sales-supporting audio (not spam)  
**Sales platforms:** Etsy digital templates + portfolio demos + custom web development  
**Status:** Scripts and queue only — **do not publish** until human review  

---

## 1. Goal

Use voiceover audio to:

1. Improve **Etsy listing video** clarity (mute-friendly + optional VO)  
2. Fuel **YouTube Shorts** and **Reels** that drive demo interest  
3. Support **portfolio project walkthroughs**  
4. Power a **homepage brand video**  
5. Enable **permissioned cold outreach voice notes** (human, short, honest)  
6. Produce **blog audio summaries** for SEO content already planned  
7. Plan **dubbing** for future non-English markets  

Audio is a **support layer** for discoverability and trust. It does **not** replace listing SEO, demos, or honest product claims.

---

## 2. What we will not do

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Celebrity voices / voice cloning without permission | Legal + trust |
| Misleading ads / fake urgency / guaranteed sales or rankings | Policy + brand |
| Robocall or spam call scripts | Abuse |
| Medical/clinical treatment claims for wellness/skincare | Compliance |
| Exact final quote claims for planners/calculators | Product honesty |
| Invented Etsy product URLs | Use placeholders only |
| Auto-publish to any platform | Human gate |

## 7. Production principles

1. **One idea per clip** — planner, calculator, CSV, or local service site.  
2. **Show UI when speaking about UI** — VO supports footage, not replaces it.  
3. **Disclaimer where needed** — preliminary estimates, educational only, not medical.  
4. **Placeholders** for URLs until real.  
5. **Consistent voice** — see `VOICE-DIRECTION.md`.  
6. **Filename discipline** — see `FILE-NAMING-SYSTEM.md`.  
7. **Queue-driven generation** — `ELEVENLABS-GENERATION-QUEUE.csv`.
