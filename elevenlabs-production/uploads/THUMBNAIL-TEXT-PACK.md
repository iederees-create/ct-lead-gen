## Design notes

- High contrast on dark charcoal or brand navy  
- Avoid cluttering with full sentences  
- Leave face/UI visible if talking-head  
- Do not overlay fake star ratings  
- For Etsy listing video (`construction-etsy-listing-video-v1.mp4`) keep text minimal—Etsy crops aggressively
