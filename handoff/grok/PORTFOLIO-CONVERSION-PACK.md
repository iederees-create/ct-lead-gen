## Extended project description

Precision Laser is a commercial website template concept for **laser cutting and engraving businesses** — not medical or cosmetic laser clinics. The experience combines a clean fabrication-focused marketing site with an interactive **Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner**.

Visitors select process type, material, thickness, size and quantity, work through file-preparation guidance, and generate a structured quote summary. Handoff paths are intended for WhatsApp and email so shop owners receive usable job details instead of one-line chat messages.

The demo brand and sample jobs are **fictional**. Planner output is **preliminary guidance only**, not a binding price. The template is designed as a static, rebrandable digital product for Etsy (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` for final implementation details).

## Business problem

Laser shops attract demand through Instagram, Google, and word of mouth — then lose time in WhatsApp threads asking for material, thickness, size, quantity, and usable files. Generic contact forms collect names and “message” fields. They do not collect **production truth**. Incomplete enquiries create slow quotes, frustrated customers, and machines waiting on art that was never cut-ready.

---

## Solution

A niche website template that markets the shop **and** qualifies work through a guided quote planner. The site educates visitors on what a serious laser brief needs, then packages the answers into a summary the business can price, reject, or request revisions on — without rewriting the same checklist every time.

---

## Quote planner explanation

The **Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner** is the product’s conversion core:

1. Capture process intent (cut / engrave / mark / mixed as implemented).  
2. Capture material and thickness.  
3. Capture dimensions and quantity.  
4. Surface file-preparation checklist items.  
5. Show a structured summary with clear **non-final** pricing posture.  
6. Handoff via WhatsApp or email (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`).

## Responsive design explanation

Layouts target phone-first shop owners and customers who photograph parts and message on mobile. Navigation, planner steps, and handoff buttons should remain usable at ~375px width without horizontal scrolling of primary content (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` with real device checks).

## Accessibility explanation

Intent: semantic headings, labelled form controls, keyboard-reachable planner steps, visible focus, and contrast-conscious themes. Exact WCAG conformance level is `VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` — do not claim AA/AAA until audited.

## Privacy explanation

Default product intent: **no vendor-side customer database** for quote submissions; data stays in the browser until the visitor sends WhatsApp/email handoff (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`). Demo pages should avoid collecting real personal data. Privacy page is a starter, not legal advice.

## Case-study narrative

**Before:** A fictional sample shop receives “Can you laser this?” with a blurry photo. Staff spend three messages collecting thickness and material, then discover the logo is a low-res JPG. Quote delayed; customer cools off.

**After (template story):** The same visitor uses the quote planner, selects acrylic, 3mm, 200×100mm, qty 50, marks file-not-ready, and sends a structured brief. Staff reply with file requirements and a real quote window — without the scavenger hunt.

**Lesson:** Conversion quality beats vanity bounce-rate metrics for fabrication SMBs.
