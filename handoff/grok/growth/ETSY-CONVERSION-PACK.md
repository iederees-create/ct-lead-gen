## How the Renovation Scope & Budget Planner works

1. Visitor opens planner on the site  
2. Enters project definition fields (type, size, finishes, trades, access, timeline, budget posture, location, contact)  
3. Tool calculates/presents preliminary scope notes and area guidance (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`)  
4. Shows configurable budget range + assumptions  
5. Surfaces trade, missing-info, and photo checklists  
6. Recommends site visit when appropriate  
7. Builds structured quote summary  
8. Hands off via WhatsApp and/or email; optional print/download (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`)  

**Always:** not a binding quotation.
