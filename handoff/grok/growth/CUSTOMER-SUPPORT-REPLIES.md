**I cannot download the files**  
Please use the Etsy purchase/download page while logged into the buying account, try another browser, and disable blockers. If the file is missing, reply with your order number and a screenshot of the download page.

**How do I open the ZIP?**  
Download the file, then unzip with your OS tools (Windows Explorer, macOS Archive Utility, or Linux `unzip`). Open START-HERE first.

No. You receive website files to host on a static host of your choice.

No.
