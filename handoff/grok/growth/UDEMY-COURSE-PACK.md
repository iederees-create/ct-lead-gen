## Course description

Learn to plan and build a construction-niche lead generation website featuring an interactive Renovation Scope & Budget Planner. You’ll cover strategy for better quote requests, responsive layout, business configuration patterns, planner logic (scope, trades, preliminary ranges), WhatsApp/email handoff patterns, accessibility and validation basics, SEO/deployment for static sites, and how commercial templates are packaged.

**Honesty module:** preliminary ranges vs binding quotations; no guaranteed leads claims.

---

## Target learner

Beginner web developers · freelance designers · HTML/CSS/JS students · developers serving local businesses · agencies building contractor sites
