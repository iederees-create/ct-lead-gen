# Day 5 — Transparency Builds Trust

**Act:** 2 (Days 3–5)  
**Theme:** Honest estimate UX  
**Publish gate:** Safe **before** Etsy publication (demo CTA)

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Position transparency — preliminary ranges, assumptions, inspection needs — as a competitive trust feature, not fine print.

---

## 2. Core story

A trustworthy construction website explains what an online estimate **can and cannot** do.

Preliminary budget ranges. Visible assumptions. Configurable rates for the business owner. Site conditions. Site inspection. No fake guarantees.

Clarity earns trust. Overclaiming earns chargebacks and angry threads.

## 4. Facebook personal-profile post

A trustworthy website explains what an estimate can and cannot do.

Online tools can help with:

• preliminary budget ranges  
• visible assumptions  
• configurable rates (set by the business)  
• flags for site conditions  

They cannot honestly replace:

• site inspection  
• drawings and specifications  
• final material selections  
• a binding quotation process  

If a construction site promises certainty without seeing the property, be suspicious.

The Southern Suburbs Builders template is built around that honesty: structure first, preliminary guidance second, real quoting still human.
