# Campaign Big Idea — Southern Suburbs Builders Website Template

**Branch:** `agent/grok-commerce`  
**Campaign window:** 7 days  
**Product:** Southern Suburbs Builders Website Template  
**Core feature:** Interactive Renovation Scope & Budget Planner  

---

## Big idea

**From vague enquiries to build-ready leads.**

Most builder websites collect noise. This campaign sells a different job: a construction website that forces clarity **before** the estimator opens WhatsApp.

---

## Narrative arc

| Act | Days | Job | Audience feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — The cost of chaos** | 1–2 | Name the pain of vague quote messages | “That’s my inbox.” |
| **2 — The planner** | 3–5 | Show the Renovation Scope & Budget Planner + trust rules | “This is how it should work.” |
| **3 — The system + launch** | 6–7 | Sell the full template as a lead-qualification system | “I can use this.” |

---

## One-sentence pitch

A responsive construction website template with an interactive Renovation Scope & Budget Planner that turns “how much to renovate?” into a structured project brief — preliminary guidance only, never a binding quotation.

---

## Who this is for

- Builders and construction companies  
- Renovation contractors  
- Extension specialists  
- Home-improvement businesses that want **better-quality** enquiries  

Not for: people seeking a full estimating ERP, guaranteed lead volume, or a fixed online price engine.

## Voice

- Direct, intelligent, practical  
- Slightly humorous  
- Trade-native, not agency-speak  
- Never desperate, never fake scarcity  

### Lines we use
- “That’s not a quote request. That’s a conversation with no data.”  
- “Price starts with scope.”  
- “Your website can qualify the lead first.”  
- “Vague enquiry in. Build-ready brief out.”  
- “Clarity earns trust.”  

### Lines we refuse
- “Guaranteed leads / sales / accuracy”  
- “Bestseller”  
- “Limited spots” / fake countdown scarcity  
- “Fully insured / certified” as product claims  
- Any invented Etsy URL
