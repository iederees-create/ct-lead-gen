## Platform notes

| Platform | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Instagram | 5–12 relevant tags; mix broad + niche |
| LinkedIn | 3–5 tags max; prefer plain language |
| X | 0–2 tags; hook does the work |
| Pinterest | Keywords in title/description > hashtag spam |
| Facebook | Minimal hashtags; conversational body |
