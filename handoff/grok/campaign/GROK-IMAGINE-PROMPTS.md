# Grok Imagine Prompts — 7-Day Campaign

**Usage:** Generate campaign stills (prefer **4:5** feed, plus **9:16** vertical crop/prompt).  
**Global bans:** no logos, no real phone numbers, no fake reviews, no bestseller/discount badges, no fake completed client project photography, no invented website UI when real screenshots are required.

When a day requires `ACTUAL_*` assets, **paste the real screenshot into Imagine as reference** and instruct: do not alter, redraw, or invent screen content.
