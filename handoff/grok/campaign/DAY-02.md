# Day 2 — The Missing Scope

**Act:** 1 (Days 1–2)  
**Theme:** A price without scope is fiction  
**Publish gate:** Safe **before** Etsy publication

---

## 1. Campaign objective

Teach the non-negotiable inputs of a useful construction enquiry so builders and customers share the same definition of “enough information.”

---

## 2. Core story

A construction price is not a personality trait. It’s an output of scope.

Without area, finish level, demolition, plumbing, electrical, structural changes, access, and timeline, any number is mostly theatre. Day 2 lists the missing pieces that turn a message into something an estimator can work with.

---

## 3. Main hook

**“A construction price means nothing without scope.”**

## 7. LinkedIn post

In professional services, incomplete intake creates rework.  
In construction, incomplete intake creates **pricing risk**.

A useful renovation or building enquiry should at least clarify:

1. Scale (area / rooms)  
2. Finish band  
3. Demolition extent  
4. Services (plumbing / electrical)  
5. Structural implications  
6. Access constraints  
7. Timeline pressure  

If a website only offers “Name / Phone / Message,” it outsources discovery to WhatsApp — and WhatsApp is a terrible form.

Day 2 of a short series on build-ready lead structure for contractors.
