# Short Video Scripts — One per Day (15–20s)

**Aspect ratio:** 9:16  
**Duration target:** 15–20 seconds  
**Music direction:** light percussive construction-adjacent bed or soft documentary piano — original / licensed stock only; **do not name copyrighted songs**  
**Global bans:** no browser bookmarks, personal tabs, private data, fake customers, fake reviews  

Days **3, 4, 6, 7** require **actual product captures**.

## Production notes

- Burn captions for silent autoplay.  
- Export H.264 MP4, under platform size limits.  
- Scrub any accidental PII from screen recordings.  
- Days 1–2 can publish pre-product; 3+ wait for real UI if claiming product fidelity.
