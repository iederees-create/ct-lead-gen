## Case-study narrative

### Problem

Small builders and renovation contractors often rely on Facebook pages, outdated brochure sites, or pure WhatsApp word-of-mouth. First-contact messages are thin:

> “How much to renovate my bathroom?”  
> “Rough price for an extension?”

Without structure, the business burns time on unqualified back-and-forth, and the homeowner gets either silence or a guess that later becomes a dispute.

Generic website templates rarely solve this. They add stock heroes and service grids, but they do not help a visitor **think through scope** before asking for a number.

### Opportunity

Create a **niche static template** that keeps setup light (no heavy CMS) while adding an original interactive tool:

**Renovation Scope & Budget Planner**

Designed to capture:

1. Project **area**  
2. **Scope** of work  
3. **Complexity** signals  
4. **Preliminary budget guidance**  
5. A **structured quote request** handoff to the business  

The commercial angle is not “AI estimates your build perfectly.” It is **honest lead structuring** with explicit non-binding language.

### Approach

- Start from the monorepo client-site lineage and rebrand as a **fictional demo business** suitable for public sale (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` final demo name treatment).  
- Centre the UX on the planner rather than vanity statistics.  
- Strip unsupported claims common in raw lead-gen pages (guaranteed workmanship, “fully insured,” invented ratings) from anything presented as fact.  
- Provide a buyer configuration surface for ordinary rebranding (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`).  
- Package as a digital download with guides, licence, and AI disclosure (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`).  
- Sell via Etsy draft workflow; portfolio shows live demo first, Etsy link only after human publish.

### What we deliberately did not build

- Binding estimating engine or materials ERP  
- Payment checkout for construction deposits  
- Fake photo portfolio of “completed jobs”  
- Guaranteed-lead marketing claims  

### Outcome (product, not client ROI)

- A sellable, category-specific template positioned on a clear differentiator  
- Listing, tags, media plan, and social pack ready for human review  
- Portfolio-ready narrative that stays accurate under compliance scrutiny  

**Do not claim:** conversion rate lifts, revenue numbers, or client testimonials unless separately measured and approved.

### Lessons for the template product line

1. **Interactive tools outperform brochure clones** in crowded Etsy template search — when the tool is real and honestly scoped.  
2. **Disclaimer quality is a product feature** for any cost-adjacent UI.  
3. **Commerce copy must lag the build** — use `VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` gates so listing claims never outrun shipped files.  
4. **Demo ethics matter** — fictional sample content must be obvious and replaceable.
