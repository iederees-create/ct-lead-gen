**Diagram steps (suggested):**

1. Land on construction website  
2. Review services / process  
3. Open Renovation Scope & Budget Planner  
4. Enter project, trades, access, photos checklist  
5. View preliminary budget range + assumptions  
6. Send structured quote request  
7. Builder inspects and issues real quotation
