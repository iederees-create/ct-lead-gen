## Production notes

1. Capture screenshots only after Claude build is stable and demo content is clearly fictional.  
2. Prefer real device frames; avoid cluttered stock workshop photos that dominate the UI.  
3. Keep text large enough to read at mobile Etsy thumbnail size on images 1–4.  
4. Do not add “Bestseller,” fake ratings, or guaranteed-leads badges.  
5. Export checklist remains `FINAL_SCREENSHOTS_PENDING` until all 10 exist.
