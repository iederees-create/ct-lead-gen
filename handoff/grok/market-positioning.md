# Market Positioning — Southern Suburbs Builders Website Template

**Product name:** Southern Suburbs Builders Website Template  
**Category:** Digital download — construction / builder / contractor website template

## One-line positioning

A responsive construction website template with an interactive **Renovation Scope & Budget Planner** that helps homeowners outline area, scope, complexity, and preliminary budget guidance — then hand off a structured quote request.

---

## Target buyers

### Primary
- Small-to-midsize **builders**, **renovation contractors**, and **home remodelers** who need a lead-ready website without a custom build.
- Freelancers / agencies packaging a **construction-niche template** for a single client (one end-business licence model — `VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` for final licence wording).

### Secondary
- Handyman-to-builder businesses expanding into renovations.
- Trade businesses that want a professional “quote first” funnel (not e-commerce).

### Not for
- Buyers needing a full CRM, estimating ERP, or binding quotation engine.
- Multi-site franchise systems without further development.
- Anyone expecting guaranteed project pricing or legal cost advice.

- Generic “construction website HTML template” (static brochure only).

- Quote-form templates without renovation-specific planning logic.

**White space we own:** renovation-specific **scope + budget planner → structured quote request** in a lightweight static package (no build tools required — `VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`).

| Risk | Rule |
