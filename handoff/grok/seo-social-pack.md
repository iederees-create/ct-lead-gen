## LinkedIn case study (short post)

**Title line:** Shipping a niche construction template around a renovation planner — not another generic brochure theme.

Most small builders still collect leads as unstructured WhatsApp messages: “How much to redo my kitchen?”

We designed a static website template whose centrepiece is an interactive **Renovation Scope & Budget Planner**. The product goal is simple:

1. Help visitors clarify area, scope, and complexity  
2. Offer **preliminary** budget guidance with explicit non-binding language  
3. Hand off a **structured quote request** to the business  

Constraints we respected:
- No fake project photography presented as real work  
- No “guaranteed leads” or inflated social proof  
- Demo identity remains fictional and buyer-replaceable  
- Estimator honesty over false precision  

Stack intent: HTML/CSS/JS, buyer configuration surface, static hosting friendly (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD` final stack notes).

If you sell services in the trades, lead quality often beats lead quantity. Tools that force clarity upstream save quoting time downstream.
