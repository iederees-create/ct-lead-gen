## Quick start (30–60 minutes)

1. Unzip the template folder.  
2. Open `START-HERE` and skim warnings.  
3. Open the configuration file (expected: `site-config.js`) in a text editor.  
4. Replace fictional business name, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, services.  
5. Configure materials, thicknesses, and planner rates/assumptions for **your** shop—or hide pricing if supported.  
6. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.  
7. Walk through the Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner on desktop and phone.  
8. Send a test WhatsApp/email handoff to yourself.  
9. Deploy the folder to a static host.  
10. Point DNS if you use a custom domain (domain not included).

## How the Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner works

**Intended visitor flow (`VERIFY_AFTER_BUILD`):**

1. Choose process type (cut / engrave / mark / combination as provided).  
2. Select material and thickness.  
3. Enter size and quantity.  
4. Complete file-preparation checklist items / status.  
5. Review summary + preliminary guidance (if enabled).  
6. Send via WhatsApp or email.

### Critical disclaimer (keep visible)

The planner creates a **structured enquiry** and may show **preliminary guidance only**.  
It is **not** a final price, binding quotation, machine-time guarantee, or stock confirmation.  
You must review artwork and capacity before committing commercial terms.
