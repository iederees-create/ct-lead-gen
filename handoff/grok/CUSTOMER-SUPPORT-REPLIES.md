```
Yes. The template is intended to ship with selectable themes and/or colour tokens in the configuration file. Choose a theme first; advanced palette edits may require CSS if you go beyond provided themes. Exact theme names are listed in the buyer guide for your package version.
```

## Can I change materials?

```
Yes. Materials offered in the Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner are intended to be configurable so they match what your shop actually cuts or engraves. Remove materials you don’t offer — demo materials are samples only.
```

---

## Can I change the quote rates?

```
Yes. Planner rates/assumptions are intended to be editable in configuration so guidance matches your market. Remember: the planner is preliminary guidance only, not a binding quotation. Always review real jobs before confirming a price.
```

---

## Does the quote planner give exact prices?

```
No — and that’s intentional. The planner builds a structured job brief and may show preliminary guidance based on the details entered and your configured assumptions. Final prices depend on artwork quality, nesting, finishing, material batch, capacity, and your shop policies. Please keep the on-page disclaimer visible.
```

## Does it send WhatsApp automatically?

```
It provides a WhatsApp handoff: the visitor’s device opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message containing the structured brief, addressed to your configured number. It does not blast messages to your customers automatically, and it is not a full CRM or marketing automation tool. Please test with your own number after you edit the config.
```
