The Surface Cleaning Estimate Planner Is Not a Binding Quote

The Surface Cleaning Estimate Planner provides a preliminary planning estimate based on the measurements, surface type, soiling condition, access difficulty, and site conditions you enter. It does not constitute a binding quote, offer, or contract. Actual cleaning time and cost vary by surface condition, access, equipment, weather, and site conditions. A professional site assessment is typically required before a firm price can be given.

Complexity Adjustments Are Starting Points, Not Guarantees

The soiling-condition and access-difficulty percentages used by the planner are general planning starting points based on common project factors, not universally exact figures for every project. Severe staining, restricted access, and multi-storey work often take longer than a straightforward, ground-level clean.

Time and Cost Estimates Are Indicative Only

Any estimated cleaning time or cost shown by the planner is indicative planning guidance only, not a purchasing specification. Coverage rates (m² per hour) vary enormously by equipment, technician experience, surface material, and site conditions. Always confirm actual time and pricing with a real site assessment before booking.
