Step 1 — Unzip the template

Extract hydro-clean-template.zip. You'll get a folder of plain HTML/CSS/JS files — no build tools or installation required.

Step 2 — Preview it locally

Double-click index.html to open it in your browser, or (recommended, for full functionality) run a simple local server from that folder, e.g. python3 -m http.server 8000, then visit http://localhost:8000/.

Step 3 — Rebrand it

Open site-config.js in any text editor and update the business name, contact details, services, pricing, testimonials, FAQs, colour theme, and Surface Cleaning Estimate Planner settings (wastage presets, material guidance rates, WhatsApp message). You should not need to touch index.html, style.css, planner.js, or app.js for ordinary changes. Full details are in COMPLETE-BUYER-GUIDE.html.

Step 4 — Test the Surface Cleaning Estimate Planner

Click through all 8 steps of the planner with sample numbers to confirm the calculations, WhatsApp handoff, and email handoff work the way you expect before you publish.

Step 5 — Publish it

Upload the folder to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or your existing web host). See the Deployment section of the Complete Buyer Guide for step-by-step instructions.
