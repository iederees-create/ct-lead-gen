# Etsy FAQ

## Is this a physical product?
No. This is a digital download; no physical item is shipped.

## What can I customise?
You can customise branding, contact details, service areas, services, colours, content and the planner pricing/coverage assumptions in `site-config.js`.

## Does it include hosting or a domain?
No. Hosting, domain registration, deployment and ongoing maintenance are not included.

## Does the planner provide a final quote?
No. It creates a preliminary estimate and structured request. The business must confirm surface suitability, scope, access, timing and price.

## Will this template guarantee leads or sales?
No. The template provides a structured enquiry experience, but no leads, rankings, enquiries or sales are guaranteed.

## Do I need coding experience?
Routine rebranding is handled in one configuration file. Basic HTML/CSS knowledge is useful for structural design changes.

## Are the legal pages legal advice?
No. They are editable starter pages and should be reviewed for the buyer’s business and jurisdiction.
