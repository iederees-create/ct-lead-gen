Quote Disclaimer

This page explains exactly what the Laser Cut & Engraving Quote Planner tool does and does not provide.

1. Preliminary Guidance Only

The planner provides preliminary guidance only. Final pricing depends on file quality, machine setup, material availability, job complexity, finishing, quantity, labour, delivery and final review.

2. What the Budget Range Is Based On

The budget range is calculated from sample material rate bands, thickness bands, cut-complexity multipliers and finish multipliers configured in site-config.js under the planner section. These are illustrative example figures, not a real pricing guarantee, and must be replaced with your own researched rates before this site is used for a real business.

3. What Can Change the Final Price

  • File quality — whether artwork arrives as a clean, cut-ready vector file
  • Machine setup and settings for the specific material and thickness
  • Material availability and any substitutions required
  • Job complexity, including nested parts and fine detail
  • Finishing requirements (polishing, painting, assembly, packaging)
  • Quantity and batch-production efficiencies or surcharges
  • Labour and delivery
  • Final review of the supplied file before production

4. File Review

Most jobs require a file review before a firm, written quotation can be issued. The Planner tool will flag when design help is likely needed based on your file-readiness answer.

5. Not a Quotation or Contract

Nothing produced by the Planner tool, including the downloadable or shareable project summary, constitutes a binding quotation, offer, or contract. A firm quotation is only issued in writing after your file has been reviewed.
