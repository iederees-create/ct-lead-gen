This guide gets your new pest-control website template running in a few minutes. It's a digital download — no physical item is shipped.

Important: Every business name, phone number, rating, statistic, and testimonial in this template is fictional sample content. You must replace it with your own accurate information before publishing a live site. See the Disclaimer page included in the template.

Step 1 — Unzip the template

Extract westlake-pest-control-template.zip. You'll get a folder of plain HTML/CSS/JS files — no build tools or installation required.

Step 2 — Preview it locally

Double-click index.html to open it in your browser, or (recommended, for full functionality) run a simple local server from that folder, e.g. python3 -m http.server 8000, then visit http://localhost:8000/.

Step 3 — Rebrand it

Open site-config.js in any text editor and update the business name, contact details, services, pricing, testimonials, FAQs and colour theme. You should not need to touch index.html, style.css, or app.js for ordinary changes. Full details are in COMPLETE-BUYER-GUIDE.html.

Step 4 — Publish it

Upload the folder to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or your existing web host). See the Deployment section of the Complete Buyer Guide for step-by-step instructions.
